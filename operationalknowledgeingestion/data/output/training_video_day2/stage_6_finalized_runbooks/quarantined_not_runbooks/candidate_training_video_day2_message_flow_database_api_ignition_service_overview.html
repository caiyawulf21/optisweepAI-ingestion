Summary

Use the Day 2 agenda items (Message Flow, Database, API, Ignition, Service) to orient where to look for operational evidence on the live OptiSweep system. The source indicates Confluence pages will be provided to break out these topics for better viewing.

When To Use

When you need to quickly orient to which live system areas correspond to the Day 2 topics—Message Flow, Database, API, Ignition, and Service—so you can gather the right operational evidence during troubleshooting.

Visual References

Procedure Steps

Step 1 — Open the provided Confluence pages for the Day 2 topics

Responsible role: L1_support

Instruction:

Open the Confluence pages that the Day 2 session indicates will be provided for better viewing of the topics.

Expected result:

You have access to Confluence sections/pages that correspond to the Day 2 topic breakdown.

Screens / Images:

Stop or Escalate If:

  • If the Confluence pages are not available or do not clearly map to live surfaces, stop and request the missing access/materials from the training owner/support lead.

Step 2 — Locate the sections corresponding to Message Flow, Database, API, Ignition, and Service

Responsible role: L1_support

Instruction:

Within the opened Confluence content, locate the sections for: Message Flow, Database, API, Ignition, and Service.

Expected result:

You can point to the Confluence sections for each of the five topics.

Stop or Escalate If:

  • If any topic section cannot be located, stop and request the missing Confluence content/access from the training owner/support lead.

Step 3 — For each topic, note which live surface the Confluence page points to

Responsible role: L1_support

Instruction:

For each topic (Message Flow, Database, API, Ignition, Service), read the Confluence section and note which live surface it points to so you know where to gather evidence.

Expected result:

You have a topic-to-live-surface mapping for all five topics that you can use during troubleshooting.

Stop or Escalate If:

  • If the Confluence pages do not clearly map to live surfaces, stop and request the missing access/materials from the training owner/support lead.
