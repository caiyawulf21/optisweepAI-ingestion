Summary

Use the “Database Walkthrough” slide segment (00:07:52–00:07:56) to confirm the training topic is a database walkthrough, so you can follow the subsequent database-related content in the OptiSweep Training Day 2 video.

When To Use

When you are watching the FORTNA OptiSweep Training Day 2 video and need to identify the database topic area being introduced by the “Database Walkthrough” segment so you can follow the next portion of the walkthrough.

Visual References

Procedure Steps

Step 1 — Locate the “Database Walkthrough” slide segment in the training video

Responsible role: L1_support

Instruction:

In the FORTNA OptiSweep Training Day 2 video, locate the slide segment at approximately 00:07:52 to 00:07:56 and view the slide frame around 00:07:54.

Expected result:

You have the slide frame visible for the segment that corresponds to “Database Walkthrough” (00:07:52.000–00:07:56.000).

Stop or Escalate If:

  • If the slide title is not visible, stop and review the correct timestamp/segment before proceeding.

Step 2 — Confirm the slide title text reads exactly “Database Walkthrough”

Responsible role: L1_support

Instruction:

Read the slide title text on the captured frame for the 00:07:52–00:07:56 segment and confirm it matches exactly “Database Walkthrough”.

Expected result:

The slide title text reads exactly “Database Walkthrough”.

Stop or Escalate If:

  • If the slide title is not visible or does not match “Database Walkthrough,” stop and review the correct timestamp/segment before proceeding.

Step 3 — Align expectations for the upcoming database walkthrough content

Responsible role: L1_support

Instruction:

After confirming the slide title is “Database Walkthrough,” use that confirmation to align your understanding of the upcoming database walkthrough content (i.e., expect the next portion to explain a database-related area).

Expected result:

You correctly identify that the training segment is a database walkthrough and can align to the database topic area being discussed in the subsequent video content.
