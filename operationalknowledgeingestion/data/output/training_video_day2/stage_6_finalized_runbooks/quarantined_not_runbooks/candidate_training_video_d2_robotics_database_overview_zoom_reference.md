## Summary

## Procedure Steps

### Step 1 — Open the “Robotics Database Overview” slide in Confluence/Miro

**Responsible role:** operator

**Instruction:**
Open the Confluence/Miro reference material that contains the slide titled “Robotics Database Overview.”

**Expected result:**
The “Robotics Database Overview” slide is visible in the Confluence/Miro viewer.

---

### Step 2 — Zoom in on the “Robotics Database Overview” slide to read text

**Responsible role:** operator

**Instruction:**
Use the Confluence/Miro zoom controls to zoom in on the “Robotics Database Overview” slide until the on-screen text is readable.

**Expected result:**
The slide text on “Robotics Database Overview” becomes readable after zooming in.

**Stop or Escalate If:**

* If the slide content is not readable even after zooming, stop and request a higher-resolution copy or a different view of the same slide from the training material owner.

---

### Step 3 — Pan/inspect the slide content as needed

**Responsible role:** operator

**Instruction:**
Use the zoom/pan controls in the Confluence/Miro viewer to inspect the “Robotics Database Overview” slide content as needed.

**Expected result:**
You can move around the zoomed slide and view the specific content you need.

---

## Success Criteria

* The “Robotics Database Overview” slide text is readable after zooming in within Confluence/Miro.

## Failure Conditions

* Slide text remains unreadable even after zooming in.
* Slide cannot be navigated/inspected with zoom/pan controls.

## Escalation Guidance

* If the slide content is not readable even after zooming, stop and request a higher-resolution copy or a different view of the same slide from the training material owner.
