Summary

Prepare the OptiSweep tipper station for pneumatic cylinder replacement by performing LOTO on the tipper, removing the station side panel (if necessary) to access the motor area, and removing the gripper cover by taking off eight (8) M8 button head screws to expose the pneumatic cylinder assembly.

When To Use

Use when replacing the tipper pneumatic cylinder and you must safely de-energize the tipper (LOTO), then remove the station side panel (if necessary) and the gripper cover to access the pneumatic cylinder assembly.

Visual References

Procedure Steps

Step 1 — Perform LOTO on the tipper

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Perform LOTO on the tipper (manual references the “Operator Station Lockout/Tagout” procedure on page 2).

Expected result:

Tipper is LOTOed.

Stop or Escalate If:

  • If LOTO cannot be completed/verified, stop and do not proceed to panel/cover removal.

Step 2 — Remove the station side panel (if necessary) to access the motor

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

If necessary, use a 3/8-in. wrench or socket to remove the nine (9) self-tapping securing the station side panel, then remove the station side panel to access the motor.

Expected result:

Station side panel is removed, exposing the motor area.

Stop or Escalate If:

  • If the side panel cannot be removed after removing the nine (9) self-tapping fasteners, stop and re-check that all required fasteners are removed before continuing.

Step 3 — Remove the gripper cover by removing eight (8) M8 button head screws

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Remove the gripper cover by removing the eight (8) M8 button head screws.

Expected result:

Gripper cover is removed after taking out the eight (8) M8 button head screws, providing access to the pneumatic cylinder assembly.

Stop or Escalate If:

  • If any M8 button head screws cannot be removed, stop and verify correct tool fit/size before applying additional force.
