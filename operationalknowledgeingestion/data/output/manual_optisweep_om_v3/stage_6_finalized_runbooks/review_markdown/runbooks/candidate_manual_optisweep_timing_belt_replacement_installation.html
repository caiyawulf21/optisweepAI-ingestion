Summary

Hands-on installation procedure for replacing an OptiSweep timing belt and setting counter-balance leg belt tension to the specified target (471 N) using equal tensioning of two screws and (optionally) a Gates 508c Sonic Tension Meter, followed by torque/fastener steps and operator-station restart.

When To Use

Use when replacing the OptiSweep timing belt and you must set the counter-balance leg belt tension to 471 N (acceptable range 448–494 N) before reassembly and restarting the operator station.

Visual References

Procedure Steps

Step 1 — Place the new timing belt on the pulleys

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Place the new timing belt on the pulleys.

Expected result:

The replacement timing belt is seated on the pulleys in the intended routing path, ready for counter-balance positioning and tensioning.

Step 2 — Position counter-balance carriage on lower hard stop

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Slightly lift the counter-balance carriage, remove the M6 fastener supporting it, and lower the counter-balance carriage to rest on the lower hard stop.

Expected result:

Counter-balance carriage is resting on the lower hard stop after the M6 fastener is removed.

Step 3 — Very lightly tighten M8x25 idler assembly screws

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Very lightly tighten the M8x25 screws holding the idler pulley assembly in place so the assembly is not loose, but it can slide under the fasteners.

Expected result:

Idler pulley assembly is held snugly (not loose) but can still slide under the M8x25 fasteners during tension adjustment.

Step 4 — Insert M6x35 tension screws to firm contact

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Insert the two M6x35 tension screws until they make firm contact with the actuator base.

Expected result:

Both M6x35 tension screws are inserted and each makes firm contact with the actuator base.

Step 5 — Equally tighten tension screws

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Equally tighten the tension screws to add tension to the belt, ensuring both screws are turned the exact same amount (same applied force).

Expected result:

Both tension screws have been advanced equally, producing balanced belt tension increase.

Step 6 — Increase tension to 471 N (448–494 N acceptable range)

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Gradually increase tension until 471 N is reached (acceptable range 448 N to 494 N).

Expected result:

Measured belt tension reaches 471 N within the acceptable range of 448–494 N.

Stop or Escalate If:

  • Stop and re-check if tension cannot be brought into the 448–494 N range.

Step 7 — Check tension only on the counter-balance leg

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Check tension only on the counter-balance leg of the belt (counter-balance is not in the way and will not interfere with belt vibration).

Expected result:

Tension measurement is taken on the counter-balance leg only.

Step 8 — Set Gates 508c Sonic Tension Meter parameters (if used)

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

If using a Gates 508c Sonic Tension Meter, set: Mass = 4.86 g/m, Width = 20 mm, Span = 1200 mm.

Expected result:

Gates 508c Sonic Tension Meter is configured with Mass 4.86 g/m, Width 20 mm, Span 1200 mm.

Step 9 — Obtain three acceptable sonic readings in a row

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Adjust/check until three acceptable readings in a row are received. Note: excessive noise (e.g., forklifts beeping, conveyors running) can interfere with sonic meter readings.

Expected result:

Three consecutive sonic tension readings are acceptable.

Stop or Escalate If:

  • Stop and re-check if three consecutive acceptable sonic meter readings cannot be obtained.
  • If excessive noise prevents stable sonic readings, reduce noise sources and re-check tension.

Step 10 — Torque M8x25 fasteners to 21.2 Nm (188 in-lbs)

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Tighten the M8x25 fasteners to 21.2 Nm (188 in-lbs) to lock the idler assembly in place.

Expected result:

M8x25 fasteners are torqued to 21.2 Nm (188 in-lbs), locking the idler assembly.

Step 11 — Raise counter-balance carriage and insert M6 socket-head screws

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Raise the counter-balance carriage back to mid-travel and insert the M6 socket-head screws for the carriage to rest against.

Expected result:

Counter-balance carriage is at mid-travel and supported/resting against the inserted M6 socket-head screws.

Step 12 — Re-attach A-axis belt clamp with M6x20 screws and Loctite®

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Re-attach the A-axis belt clamp using the six M6x20 screws and Loctite®, ensuring the belt clamp aligns with belt teeth and does not pinch the belt (belt may need slight movement to line up).

Expected result:

A-axis belt clamp is reattached with six M6x20 screws using Loctite®, aligned with belt teeth, and belt is not pinched.

Stop or Escalate If:

  • Stop and re-check clamp alignment if the belt appears pinched or the belt clamp does not align with belt teeth.

Step 13 — Re-attach counter-balance belt clamp with M6x20 screws and Loctite®

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Re-attach the counter-balance belt clamp using the six M6x20 screws and Loctite®, ensuring alignment with belt teeth and no belt pinch (counter-balance may need to be lifted slightly for alignment).

Expected result:

Counter-balance belt clamp is reattached with six M6x20 screws using Loctite®, aligned with belt teeth, and belt is not pinched.

Stop or Escalate If:

  • Stop and re-check clamp alignment if the belt appears pinched or the belt clamp does not align with belt teeth.

Step 14 — Remove M6 socket-head screws supporting A-axis and counter-balance

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Remove the M6 socket-head screws supporting the A-axis and counter-balance.

Expected result:

The M6 socket-head screws that supported the A-axis and counter-balance are removed.

Step 15 — Reinstall side panels

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Reinstall the side panels.

Expected result:

Side panels are reinstalled.

Step 16 — Restart the operator station

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Re-start the operator station (referenced as “Starting The Operator Station” on page 66).

Expected result:

Operator station is restarted per the manual’s referenced restart procedure.
