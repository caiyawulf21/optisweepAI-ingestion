Summary

Use the OptiSweep Training Day 1 overview segment to set expectations for which systems will be covered next: Geek Plus (via Chrome) and/or Aveva (via Jose).

When To Use

When you need a quick orientation during OptiSweep Training Day 1 to understand the scope of the upcoming overview portion and which named systems (Geek Plus and/or Aveva) will be covered next.

Procedure Steps

Step 1 — Locate the “Overview What is Optisweep?” slide frame in the Training Day 1 video segment.

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

In the UPSFORTNA OptiSweep Training Day 1 video, navigate to the segment timestamp range 00:03:11.000 to 00:03:28.000 and locate the slide frame whose OCR reads “Overview What is Optisweep?”.

Expected result:

You can see the slide frame with OCR text “Overview What is Optisweep?” during the 00:03:11.000–00:03:28.000 segment.

Stop or Escalate If:

  • If you cannot identify the “Overview What is Optisweep?” frame, ask the trainer for clarification rather than guessing the scope.

Step 2 — Read/confirm the segment statement that the presenter will “go through just the overview.”

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Within the same 00:03:11.000–00:03:28.000 segment, confirm the presenter’s statement that they will “go through just the overview.”

Expected result:

You can identify the presenter’s statement that the session will cover “just the overview.”

Stop or Escalate If:

  • If you cannot identify the “Overview What is Optisweep?” frame or the Geek Plus/Aveva statements, ask the trainer for clarification rather than guessing the scope.

Step 3 — Note the stated topic coverage: Geek Plus (Chrome) and/or Aveva (Jose).

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Within the 00:03:11.000–00:03:28.000 segment, note the presenter’s conditional statements: if you don’t know the system, “my Chrome will go through Geek Plus,” and if Jose shows up, “he’ll do Aveva,” otherwise “I’ll do it.”

Expected result:

You can state which system(s) are likely to be covered next based on the presenter’s Geek Plus/Aveva statements (Geek Plus via Chrome and/or Aveva via Jose).

Stop or Escalate If:

  • If the trainee cannot identify the “Overview What is Optisweep?” frame or the Geek Plus/Aveva statements, ask the trainer for clarification rather than guessing the scope.

Step 4 — Use the segment to set expectations for what system topic(s) will be covered next.

Responsible role: operator

Instruction:

Based on the segment’s conditional statements, decide which system topic(s) you should expect next during the overview portion: Geek Plus (Chrome) and/or Aveva (Jose).

Expected result:

You can explain that the session is an overview and that Geek Plus and Aveva are the named systems likely to be covered next.

Stop or Escalate If:

  • If your scope understanding is uncertain, ask the trainer for clarification rather than guessing the scope.
