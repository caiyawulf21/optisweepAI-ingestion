## Summary

Preventive maintenance for an OptiSweep tipper: visually inspect the Z-axis linear rails for grease wetness or dried grease. If the rails are dry, lubricate the bearing blocks through the grease nipples using automotive bearing grease, applying 1.0 to 3.0 cc per bearing block. Do not rely on wiping grease on the rails as a substitute for lubrication.

## When To Use

Use during tipper preventive maintenance to check Z-axis linear rail lubrication condition (wet grease vs dried grease) and to apply grease to the Z-axis bearing blocks when the rails are dry.

## Procedure Steps

### Step 1 — Visually inspect both Z-axis linear rails for grease wetness or dried grease

**Responsible role:** operator

**Instruction:**
Visually inspect both Z-axis linear rails and look for wet grease and/or signs of dried grease.

**Expected result:**
You can classify the condition of the Z-axis linear rails as either wet with grease or dry (not wet with grease) / showing dried grease.

---

### Step 2 — If rails are dry, lubricate the bearing blocks using automotive bearing grease

**Responsible role:** operator

**Instruction:**
If the rails are dry (not wet with grease), lubricate the bearing blocks using automotive bearing grease (e.g., Mobil1™).

**Expected result:**
Grease is applied to the bearing blocks via the grease nipples using the specified quantity per block (1.0 to 3.0 cc per block).

---

### Step 3 — Locate the grease nipples on the two bearing blocks

**Responsible role:** operator

**Instruction:**
Locate the grease nipples: there are two (2) bearing blocks, each with one standard 1/4-in nipple on each end (four (4) nipples total).

**Expected result:**
You identify four (4) grease nipples total: two bearing blocks, one 1/4-in nipple on each end of each block.

---

### Step 4 — Apply 1.0 to 3.0 cc of grease to each bearing block

**Responsible role:** operator

**Instruction:**
Using a grease applicator, add 1.0 to 3.0 cc of grease to each bearing block (via its grease nipples).

**Expected result:**
Total grease applied per bearing block is within 1.0 to 3.0 cc, and grease has been delivered through the bearing block nipples.

---

### Step 5 — Do not substitute wiping grease on the rails for lubrication

**Responsible role:** operator

**Instruction:**
Do not rely on wiping grease on the rails as a substitute for lubrication.

**Expected result:**
Lubrication is performed by greasing the bearing blocks via the grease nipples (not by wiping rails).

---

## Success Criteria

- Both Z-axis linear rails are inspected and classified as wet/dried or dry.
- If rails are dry, grease is applied to each bearing block via the four (4) 1/4-in grease nipples with 1.0 to 3.0 cc per block.
- Lubrication is performed via nipples; wiping grease on rails is not used as the lubrication method.

## Failure Conditions

- Rails are dry (not wet with grease) and bearing blocks are not lubricated via nipples.
- Lubrication is attempted by wiping rails instead of greasing bearing blocks.
- Rails show signs of dried grease but lubrication cannot be performed as specified due to inability to access nipples.

## Escalation Guidance

- If lubrication cannot be performed as specified (e.g., inability to access nipples), stop and escalate to maintenance support.
