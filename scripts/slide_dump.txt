Optisweep Ingestion Pipeline 
https://github.com/caiyawulf21/optisweepAI-ingestion
Why: Original data sources are useful to people but are not directly usable by a RAG assistant unless they are broken into reliable, source-linked records. In order to allow the Optisweep knowledge to expand as the product matures, a standardized repeatable ingestion pipeline must be constructed.

Structure: LangGraph Agent where every node represents a data ingestion stage (denoted on the following slide).

What it Produces: stable operational information chunks, enriched visual artifacts and runbooks.
